About Us

The Story Assisted Living Training Institute is a project created by Memory Care and Caregiving experts with years of experience in their field. Our mission is to deliver participants the knowledge and tools they need to excel in providing exceptional care services.


Jennifer is the creator of this assisted living training program. With over a decade of experience in caregiving and memory care, she has dedicated her career to improving the lives of seniors and those with memory impairments. Her passion for education and commitment to excellence have driven her to develop comprehensive training programs that empower caregivers to provide the highest quality of care.
